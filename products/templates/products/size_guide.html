How to Measure

Body Measurements
  • Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest/bust, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 7-9 inches below your waist.
  • Sleeve Length: Measure from the center back of your neck to your wrist with your arm slightly bent.
Measuring Tips
  • Use a flexible measuring tape
  • Measure over undergarments or form-fitting clothes
  • Keep the tape snug but not tight
  • Take measurements at the end of the day when your body is at its largest
  • Have someone help you for more accurate measurements

Size Recommendations

Between Sizes?

If you're between sizes, we generally recommend:

  • For a loose fit: Choose the larger size
  • For a fitted look: Choose the smaller size
  • For activewear: Choose your exact measurements
